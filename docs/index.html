For the Agents You Can Audit track, permission comes before action. MongoDB Atlas holds the identities, supplier and policy revisions, verification, and approval used by the server to decide whether a payment may run.

Read the request

The model extracts the invoice and looks up the saved supplier. Email text can't change the trusted contact or grant approval.

Check and approve

A separately signed-in person checks the trusted contact, then approves the exact invoice revision, amount, and destination. The server binds that permission to the current supplier and policy revisions.

Execute and check

A separate HTTP service records the simulated payment. PayProof matches its receipt to the approved details and saves the result.

Follow one invoice
from email to evidence.

These are actual screens from a synthetic demo for Maple Ridge Construction and Cedar Supply Co. Open any screenshot to read it at full size.

1 · Finance operator

Start with the supplier email.

Sign in as the operator, paste the invoice email, and choose Process invoice. The model looks up the supplier and proposes the payment.

This example asks for CAD 4,800, a new account ending in 7719, and an urgent payment. The saved account ends in 0042, so the server puts it on hold.

When repeating a demo, change the invoice number. Duplicate supplier invoice numbers are intentionally rejected.

2 · Separate payment approver

Use the contact you already trust.

Sign in separately as the approver. Check the change through the saved supplier contact, then choose Record verification and Approve this payment.

The agent can't approve its own request. The email's replacement phone number doesn't become the trusted contact.

The contact and confirmation shown here are a synthetic role-play. No phone call was made.

3 · Change the payment details

An approval has a precise target.

$4,800→$4,900

Use Amend payment details and save an amendment. Changing the amount or destination clears the previous verification and approval.

The old revision can't be dispatched, even with a direct API request. The actual server response in this demo was Payment revision changed.

4 · Execute and inspect

Approve again.
Pay once.
Read the trail.

Return to the correct amount, record a fresh check, and approve. As the operator, choose Run payment agent.

The demo produced 1 simulated ledger entry and 13 recorded actions. The receipt matched the approved payment details.

Atlas keeps permission
and evidence in reach.

Invoice documents hold verification, approval, operation IDs, and the action timeline. Separate collections store suppliers, identities, and denied security events.

Atomic updates keep invoice state and its timeline together. Before dispatch, a transaction checks current authority and revisions. The simulator uses one ledger ID per tenant and operation to prevent duplicate entries.

What you're looking at

Simulated
Payments recorded in a separate service. No bank connection, funds moved, or emails sent.

This is an application-level audit trail. Database administrators can change stored data; the demo doesn't claim tamper-proof records or complete fraud detection.
